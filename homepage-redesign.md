# Halden Supply Homepage Redesign (CRO-Optimized for Immediate Purchase)

## Goal
Design a high-converting Shopify homepage that drives **immediate checkout/purchase** for SecureView.

---

## 1) Information Architecture (Top to Bottom)

1. Announcement Bar (Offer + urgency)
2. Hero (single product value proposition + primary CTA)
3. Trust/Assurance Strip (shipping, returns, secure checkout)
4. Product Purchase Module (price, benefits, ATC, express checkout)
5. “Why It Converts” Benefits Section
6. Social Proof (star summary + verified customer proof)
7. How It Works (3 steps)
8. Feature Deep Dive (objection handling by use case)
9. Comparison Table (vs alternatives)
10. FAQ (purchase objections)
11. Final CTA Section (offer reminder + checkout button)
12. Footer (policy links, contact, brand story consistency)

## 3) Shopify Theme Implementation Notes

- Keep header navigation minimal on homepage to reduce exits.
- Use sticky ATC on mobile after first product module scroll.
- Ensure dynamic checkout buttons are active and visible on homepage featured product.
- Place shipping/returns/warranty directly under purchase CTAs.
- Keep messaging consistent across hero, product section, and footer (avoid mixed brand positioning).
